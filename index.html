Haoze Song  

Contact: hzsong[AT]nus.edu.sg

I am currently a Research Fellow at the School of Computing, National University of Singapore (NUS), working with Prof. Bingsheng He. My research interests lie in distributed systems and currently focus on LLM System and Cloud Computing. I obtained my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Hong Kong (HKU) in 2025, where I was advised by Prof. Heming Cui.
Prior to my Ph.D., I received my Bachelor of Engineering degree from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) in 2020 with outstanding graduate honours.


Research Statement

My research interests broadly lie in distributed systems, spanning cloud computing, distributed consensus, fault-tolerant computing, real-time analysis, and large-scale intelligence. I enjoy conducting fundamental research. I am driven to contribute to cutting-edge work that has a tangible, positive impact on our lives.
